# Week 01 — Entra Identity Administration Foundations

## Overview

This week establishes the foundational identity administration skills required for Microsoft Entra ID and the SC-300 Identity and Access Administrator certification.

The week focuses on understanding the Entra tenant, administrative roles, delegated administration, administrative units, domains, and tenant-wide identity settings.

The labs are designed to demonstrate these concepts through hands-on work in a dedicated Microsoft Entra lab environment.

---

## Week 01 Objectives

By the end of Week 01, I will be able to:

- Identify the structure of a Microsoft Entra tenant.
- Understand Microsoft Entra administrative roles.
- Understand role permissions and least-privilege administration.
- Understand Administrative Units and delegated administration.
- Identify and verify custom domains.
- Understand tenant-wide identity settings.
- Document an Entra environment using screenshots and technical documentation.
