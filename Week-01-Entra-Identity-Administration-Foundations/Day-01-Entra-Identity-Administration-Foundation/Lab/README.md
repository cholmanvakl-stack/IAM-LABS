Step 2 — Lab Objective
Today's lab is going to demonstrate that you can work with the foundational Entra administration concepts you just studied.
We'll cover:
1. Tenant configuration
2. Company branding
3. Microsoft Entra roles
4. Administrative Units
5. Role permissions
6. Custom domains
7. Tenant-wide settings
And we're going to use your existing IAM LABS tenant.
