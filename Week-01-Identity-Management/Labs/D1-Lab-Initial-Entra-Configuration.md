# D1 Lab — Initial Microsoft Entra ID Configuration

## Goal
Establish and document the tenant baseline without making unnecessary production-style changes.

## Tasks
1. Record tenant properties and tenant ID.
2. Review company branding.
3. Review Microsoft Entra roles and role assignments.
4. Create or review Administrative Units and identify their intended scope.
5. Review role permissions for delegated administration.
6. Review custom domains.
7. Review tenant-wide external/collaboration settings.

## Validation
- Tenant identity is documented.
- Role assignments are understood.
- Administrative Unit scope is understood.
- Domain and tenant-wide settings are recorded.
