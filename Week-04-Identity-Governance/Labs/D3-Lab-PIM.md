# D3 Lab — Privileged Identity Management

## Goal
Reduce standing privilege and demonstrate just-in-time administration.

## Tasks
1. Review privileged role assignments.
2. Convert a suitable test assignment to eligible access where supported.
3. Configure activation requirements.
4. Test activation.
5. Review PIM for Azure resources.
6. Review privileged access groups.
7. Review audit history.
8. Document emergency/break-glass account principles.

## Validation
Explain eligible vs active, activation, MFA/approval, scope, and audit.

## Safety
Do not remove the only administrator or emergency access path.

## Troubleshooting
**Role → Eligibility → Activation → MFA/Approval → Scope → Action → Audit**
