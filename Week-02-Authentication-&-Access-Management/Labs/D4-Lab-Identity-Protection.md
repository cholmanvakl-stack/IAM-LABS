# D4 Lab — Identity Protection

## Goal
Investigate identity risk and connect risk signals to access decisions and remediation.

## Tasks
1. Review Identity Protection fundamentals.
2. Distinguish user risk from sign-in risk.
3. Review risk detections and risky users.
4. Review current risk-based Conditional Access implementation.
5. Review MFA registration requirements.
6. Investigate a test risk event if available.
7. Review workload identity protection.
8. Review Microsoft Defender for Identity.
9. Review the Identity Risk Management Agent.

## Current Implementation Note
Legacy Identity Protection user-risk/sign-in-risk policies are being retired in 2026; learn the terminology, but use current Conditional Access risk conditions for modern implementation.

## Validation
Explain detection → evidence → policy → authentication/remediation.

## Troubleshooting
**User → Risk Type → Detection → Evidence → Policy → Authentication → Remediation → Verify**
