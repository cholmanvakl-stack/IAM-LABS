# Remediate threats using Microsoft Defender

## Main concept
`Microsoft Defender for Office 365` helps detect, investigate, and remediate malicious email and collaboration threats.

**Official Microsoft Learn module:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/m365-threat-remediate/

## Core knowledge
- `Threat Explorer and investigation workflows`
- `automated investigation and response`
- `Safe Links`
- `Safe Attachments`
- `anti-phishing policies`
- `phishing triage agent`
- `attack simulation training`

## How concepts connect
Email protection policies reduce exposure; alerts and investigation reveal what was delivered or clicked; response removes or contains threats; simulations measure readiness.

## SC-200 exam decisions
Differentiate Safe Links from Safe Attachments; distinguish prevention from post-delivery remediation; recognize simulated attacks versus actual incidents.

## Troubleshooting
Check policy scope → message delivery and verdict → user actions → investigation/automation results → remediation status → follow-up.

## Check understanding
1. A malicious message reached several mailboxes. What evidence and actions would you check before closing the incident?
2. Which Defender product or portal view would you start with, and why?
3. What evidence would prove the response worked?

**Study tip:** Explain the answer in terms of `signal → scope → investigation → response → validation`, not just feature definitions.
